Education Institutions Insurance

Running a school, college, or educational trust comes with significant responsibilities toward students, teaching and non-teaching staff, campus infrastructure, and daily operations. Our Education Institutions Insurance advisory and coverage solutions are tailored specifically to safeguard K-12 schools, residential campuses, and higher education institutions against unforeseen medical, accidental, operational, and property risks—ensuring complete peace of mind for school management and parents.

Staff Insurance

Comprehensive health, life, and accidental protection designed for teachers, administrative personnel, transport drivers, and support staff to enhance employee welfare and retention.

  • Group Health & Hospitalization Insurance (GMC) for teaching & non-teaching staff
  • Group Personal Accident (GPA) coverage for on-campus and commute incidents
  • Group Term Life Insurance & maternity/family floater add-on options
  • Cashless hospitalization network & dedicated claim settlement support

Student's Insurance

Dedicated student safety and protection plans covering learners inside the campus, on school buses, during sports events, and on domestic or international educational trips.

  • Student Group Personal Accident & emergency medical expense coverage
  • School Fee Protection / Education Continuity cover in case of parent/guardian loss
  • Sports, laboratory, and school excursion / field-trip accident protection
  • Affordable annual per-student plans with fast cashless or reimbursement claims

General Insurance

Broad-spectrum operational and liability insurance protecting your institution's fleet, public responsibilities, leadership, and day-to-day administrative operations.

  • School Bus & Institutional Fleet Motor Insurance with passenger liability cover
  • Public & Third-Party Liability Insurance for campus visitors and events
  • Fidelity Guarantee & Cash-in-Transit / Cash-in-Safe protection
  • Cyber & Digital ERP Data Risk coverage for institutional records

Property Insurance

Robust asset and infrastructure protection for school buildings, smart classrooms, STEM & robotics labs, libraries, auditoriums, and hostel facilities.

  • Standard Fire & Special Perils (SFSP) cover for academic blocks & hostels
  • Natural calamity protection (flood, storm, earthquake, lightning)
  • Electronic Equipment, Smart Boards, Computer & Robotics Lab Machinery cover
  • Burglary, theft, and campus furniture & library asset protection
