School Audit and Compliance

Running a school means meeting a long list of rules from education boards, government departments and safety authorities. Our School Audit and Compliance service gives you a clear, independent picture of where your school stands and what needs attention.
We carry out a detailed audit of every key area: academics and curriculum delivery, teacher qualifications, student records, admissions, fee structures, infrastructure, fire and building safety, transport, health and hygiene, and child protection policies.
Our team checks your school against the norms of CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, IB and state boards, along with RTE and other government regulations. We review the documents, licences and approvals you need and make sure they are complete and up to date.
After the audit, you receive a clear report with findings, risk levels and a practical action plan. We then support your team in closing the gaps, preparing for board inspections and affiliation renewals, and setting up systems that keep you compliant year after year.
With our support, your school can avoid penalties and last-minute surprises, build trust with parents and authorities, and focus on delivering quality education.
