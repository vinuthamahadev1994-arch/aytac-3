Other Services (SATS, PEN & H-DISK)

Beyond core school setup and academic consulting, AYTAC Global Educational Solutions provides specialized administrative, government-portal compliance, and institutional digital data support services—ensuring your school stays 100% compliant, digitally organized, and audit-ready throughout the academic year.

SATS (Student Achievement Tracking System)

End-to-end management and operational support for the Karnataka Student Achievement Tracking System (SATS) portal, helping schools maintain accurate and timely student records.

  • New student enrollment, admission entry & SATS ID generation
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) issuance, import & inter-school transfers
  • Daily attendance, CCE/marks entry & academic promotion updates
  • Error resolution, data verification & department compliance reporting

PEN (Permanent Education Number)

Complete assistance for UDISE+ Permanent Education Number (PEN) generation, validation, and student lifecycle mapping in line with Ministry of Education norms.

  • UDISE+ portal onboarding & unique 11-digit PEN allocation
  • Student Aadhaar, demographic & APAAR/ABC ID verification
  • Bulk student data migration, duplicate resolution & dropout tracking
  • Board exam & scholarship eligibility synchronization

H-DISK (Institutional Digital Storage & Data Support)

Reliable digital record archiving, high-capacity campus data storage solutions, and IT hardware disk management tailored for schools and educational trusts.

  • Centralized archiving of student, staff, exam & statutory documents
  • Automated local & hybrid backup setup to prevent data loss
  • Secure storage organization for CCTV, lab & administrative systems
  • Ongoing maintenance, recovery readiness & campus IT support
