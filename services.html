Meet Our Experts

School Establishment

We provide turn-key assistance for the establishment of national and international board K-12 schools including CBSE, ICSE, Cambridge, and IB.

School Improvement

We undertake school improvement assignments streamlining academics, infrastructure, Training, upgrade school brand position, thereby increasing the rate of admissions.

School Management

We assist promoters of national and international schools to manage their day and residential institutions by streamlining and automation of school functions.

School Affiliation

We specialize in guiding educational institutions through the intricate process of obtaining affiliations with esteemed educational boards such as CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, or IB in India.

School Trip

Curriculum-aligned day-outs, domestic and international educational tours that inspire, educate and transform students.

Educational Software (Smart Education ERP)

Robotic Classes

New School Turn-key Project

School Training Support
